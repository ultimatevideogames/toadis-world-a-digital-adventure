# Privacy Policy — Toadi’s World

**Last updated: 13 November 2026**

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Toadi’s World (“the Game”) handles information when you use the application.

The Game is designed to be played without creating an account or providing personal information.

## 2. Information Collected by the Game

The Game does not collect or transmit personal information to servers operated by the developer.

The Game does not include third-party advertising, advertising tracking or analytics services.

The Game does not require access to your contacts, photos, microphone or location.

## 3. Local Game Data

The Game stores information locally to support gameplay, including:

- Completed levels and unlocked characters.
- Scores and arcade challenge progress.
- Game preferences and audio settings.
- Local arcade records, including any locally saved player name.

Arcade minigames may use local web storage within the application. This storage supports the games and does not provide unrestricted internet browsing.

Local game data is not uploaded to a developer-operated account or cloud-save service.

## 4. Data Retention and Removal

Local game data remains available until it is removed by the application, the operating system or the user.

Deleting the app generally removes its local data. Apple device backups may retain or restore application data according to your device settings.

The developer cannot remotely access or delete saves stored on your device.

## 5. Apple Services

The Game uses standard Apple technologies for functions such as graphics, audio, local storage and controller support.

Apple may process App Store, device or diagnostic information according to your settings and Apple’s own privacy policies. Device backup and restoration are also managed through Apple’s services and settings.

## 6. Support Requests and GitHub

If you choose to contact us through GitHub, you may voluntarily provide information such as your GitHub username, device details, a problem description or screenshots.

We use the information you submit to respond to your request and investigate reported problems.

GitHub hosts the support repository and handles information submitted through its services under its own privacy policies.

GitHub issues may be public. Do not post passwords, payment information or other sensitive personal information.

## 7. Children’s Privacy

The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from children or require children to create an account.

A parent or guardian can contact us through the support page with privacy questions. Please avoid including a child’s personal information in a public issue.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the Game or its information-handling practices change.

The latest policy will be published on this page, with an updated revision date.

## 9. Contact

For support or privacy questions, please visit the home page

Please do not include sensitive personal information in a public support request.
