# Toadi’s World — Support

Welcome to Toadi’s World!

Join Toadi and friends for a colourful pixel-art adventure filled with platforming, boss battles, arcade games and mushroom pizza.

Created by LDM.

## How to Play

- Use the arrows to move.
- Press **A** to jump. Hold it for a higher jump.
- Press **B** to fire.
- Use the lightning button during boss fights when your special attack is ready.
- Collect mini pizza slices for points.
- Collect full pizzas to restore health.

To choose a level using touch controls, tap it once to select it, then tap the same level again to confirm.

Compatible Bluetooth controllers are also supported.

## How Do I Pause?

Long-press the play area to open the pause menu.

With a compatible controller, press the controller’s Menu button.

## How Do I Unlock More Content?

Complete story levels to unlock further adventures and characters.

After completing Level 7:

1. Play **Arcade Night**.
2. Clear all four cabinet challenges and finish the arcade farewell.
3. Enter **Special Stage**, playable only as Sashton & Chippy.
4. Complete Special Stage to unlock the duo for other levels.

## How Does the Carnival Coaster Work?

Walk to the boarding station and press **A / Jump** to board.

The coaster moves automatically. Press **A / Jump** to clear broken track. Missing a jump returns you to the station.

## Does the Game Need an Internet Connection?

The game and its arcade minigames are designed to run locally on your device. No account is required.

An internet connection is needed to access this support page or submit a GitHub issue.

## Is Progress Saved?

Progress, character unlocks and scores are saved locally.

Deleting the app may remove its saved progress. Device backups and restoration depend on your Apple settings.

## Report a Problem

Please use Github for bugs, questions or feedback.

Include:

- Your iPhone or iPad model.
- Your iOS or iPadOS version.
- The game version.
- The affected level or arcade game.
- What happened and what you expected.
- Steps to reproduce the problem.
- A screenshot, if helpful.

GitHub issues may be public. Please do not include passwords, payment information or other sensitive personal information.
